## 1. United States

### 1.1 Seed: administrative levels

| rank | level | government type | census unit | what is a government |

| 2 | state | provincial_government | SUMLEV 040 | 50 states + DC + PR (+ AS, GU, MP, VI if wanted). Names: "State of X" (46), "Commonwealth of" KY/MA/PA/VA/PR, "Government of the District of Columbia". |

| 4 | municipality | municipal_government | SUMLEV 162 (places) and SUMLEV 061 (county subdivisions) | Incorporated places with FUNCSTAT A (19,469); towns/townships with FUNCSTAT A (16,147), which exist only in 20 states: CT, IL, IN, KS, MA, ME, MI, MN, MO, ND, NE, NH, NJ, NY, OH, PA, RI, SD, VT, WI. |

The `provincial_government` and `regional_government` descriptions in `shared.py` already say "state" and "county", so no new types are needed for governments. `department` says "a department of the national government"; US state agencies are also "departments", so its description should widen to "national or state", or US state agencies go in as `agency`.

Authoritative source for "which units are governments", state by state: Census Bureau, *Individual State Descriptions: 2022 Census of Governments* (PDF, 2.6 MB): https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2024/econ/2022ISD.pdf

Functional status codes (https://www.census.gov/library/reference/code-lists/functional-status-codes.html): A active government; B active, partially consolidated; C active, consolidated with another government; F fictitious filler; G subordinate; I inactive; N nonfunctioning legal entity; S statistical. Rule: A, B, C are governments; F, N, S, I are not.

### 1.3 Places list: exceptions (the Ontario "separated cities" equivalent)

1. **Independent cities as county equivalents**: 38 in Virginia + Baltimore city, St. Louis city, Carson City (+ DC). They appear three times (SUMLEV 050 FUNCSTAT F; SUMLEV 162 FUNCSTAT A, CLASSFP C7; SUMLEV 061 CLASSFP Z7). Keep the place row as the one government; the county code becomes a second identifier of the same place or is dropped.
2. **Consolidated city-counties**, two patterns. (a) SUMLEV 170 consolidated city + a "(balance)" place (CLASSFP C8, FUNCSTAT F) + small separately incorporated places inside that stay governments: Indianapolis-Marion, Louisville-Jefferson, Nashville-Davidson, Athens-Clarke, Augusta-Richmond, Butte-Silver Bow, Greeley County KS, Milford CT. The government is the 170 row; the "(balance)" row is not. (b) County FUNCSTAT C + ordinary place FUNCSTAT A: San Francisco, Denver, Broomfield, Philadelphia, Jacksonville/Duval, New Orleans/Orleans, Anchorage, Juneau, Sitka, Wrangell, Lexington-Fayette, Columbus-Muscogee, Macon-Bibb, Kansas City-Wyandotte, the five NYC boroughs (one government: New York city), and several small GA/TN/MT counties. Keep the place; the county row is the same institution. **Honolulu** is county-only (H6/A; "City and County of Honolulu"; Urban Honolulu is a CDP): promote the county to municipal_government.
3. **Coextensive place/MCD duplicates** in the 20 MCD states: a city appears as a 162 place (A) and a 061 MCD row (C5/C2 → F, or T5 → C). Drop the F rows; merge T5 rows with the place; keep T1/A towns and townships as distinct governments even when villages (162) sit inside them (NY, WI, MI, OH, VT: both are governments).
4. **Nonfunctioning and statistical MCDs**: Z1/N (8,159 rows: AR, IA, IL part, LA, MD, MO, MS, NC, NE part, TN, VA, WV, PR barrios), Z5/S census county divisions (5,235), Z3/S unorganized territory (300), T9/I inactive townships (227). Filter 061 to FUNCSTAT A/B/C.
5. **Connecticut**: counties abolished 1960; since 2022 the county equivalents are 9 planning regions (FIPS 09110–09190, FUNCSTAT N, no website) and the 169 towns are the governments. The 2020 ANSI files still use the old county codes 09001–09015; SUB-EST2025 and the 2026 Gazetteer use the new ones.
6. **Massachusetts and Rhode Island**: 9 MA counties and all 5 RI counties are FUNCSTAT N. MA keeps 5 county governments (Barnstable, Bristol, Dukes, Norfolk, Plymouth); Nantucket is counted as a town.
7. **Alaska**: the 11 census areas of the Unorganized Borough are FUNCSTAT S; organized boroughs are the county governments; "Anchorage Municipality", "Juneau City and Borough" follow pattern 2b.
8. **DC**: a state row (040 A), a county row (050 F) and "Washington city" (162 N). One institution at the state level; suppress the other two.
9. **Places spanning counties**: 162 rows carry COUNTY=000; SUMLEV 157 rows give the county parts with PRIMGEO_FLAG=1 on the primary county. Parent = primary county.
10. **Louisiana** East Baton Rouge and Lafayette parishes are FUNCSTAT B (partially consolidated): keep parish and city both.
11. **Kalawao County HI** (FUNCSTAT G, administered by the state health department) is not a buyer.
12. **Name collisions** are everywhere (seven "Jacksonville city", "Richmond city" in six states): key on state + code, never on name alone.

### 1.4 Naming rules

Designator groups (legal form puts the designator first): [City], [Town], [Township, Charter Township, Civil Township, Metro Township], [Village], [Borough], [County], [Parish], [Plantation], [Municipality], [City and County, City and Borough], [Consolidated Government, Unified Government, Metropolitan Government, Metro Government, Urban County Government, City-Parish], [State, Commonwealth], [Territory]. Connectors: "of", "and", "/". Leading: "The".

Census NAME forms put a lowercase type after the name: "Springfield city", "Springfield town", "Canton charter township", "Cyr plantation", "Anchorage municipality", "Juneau city and borough", "Lexington-Fayette urban county", "Indianapolis city (balance)", "Carson City" (no suffix), "Urban Honolulu CDP", "Autaugaville CCD", "Capáez barrio". County names are Title Case: "Cook County", "Orleans Parish", "Fairbanks North Star Borough", "Yukon-Koyukuk Census Area", "Richmond city" (VA), "Adjuntas Municipio", "Capitol Planning Region". The existing `Naming.core()` already strips a trailing designator ("X city" → "x") and a leading "City of", so both forms meet; the list module composes the legal name from the census type ("X city" → "City of X") and takes the Govt_Units UNIT_NAME when it matches.

### 1.5 Federal and state institutions

- **Federal Register agencies API**: https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/agencies.json (473 agencies; `name, short_name, parent_id, child_ids, agency_url` = official website). Best seed for departments and agencies; includes some defunct bodies, filter by description or recent activity. Cross-check: `current-federal.csv` from the .gov registry (1,316 federal domains with branch). The U.S. Government Manual has bulk XML (https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata/GOVMAN). SAM.gov's Federal Hierarchy API needs a key and allows 10 requests a day for non-federal users (UNVERIFIED response).
- **State governments**: no single official list with domains; the .gov registry filtered to "State or territory" plus a hand-written anchor per state (government name, domains such as `alabama.gov`/`al.gov`). Every state publishes an "agencies A–Z" page but there is no cross-state file; state agencies would be one small list module per state later.

Region-level governments exist only in QC (MRC), BC (RD) and ON. Everywhere else the CD is census-only and municipalities sit under the province, as Ontario's CDR divisions do today.

Exceptions the census shows (computed from the files):

- **Nova Scotia** is the hard one. The 9 "Municipality of the County of X" governments (Annapolis, Kings, Colchester, Cumberland, Pictou, Antigonish, Inverness, Richmond, Victoria) have no CSD of their own: each is 2 to 4 `SC` subdivisions ("Kings, Subd. A/B/C/D") inside a CTY division that also holds the towns. The 11 district municipalities (`MD`) and 4 regional municipalities are proper CSDs. Rule: the 9 county municipalities take their division's code, level municipality, parent province, population summed over the SC parts; the towns inside sit under the province, not the county (NS is single-tier). Four CD/CSD name pairs collide ("Shelburne" MD vs T, likewise Yarmouth, Digby, Lunenburg): the directory match needs the designator rule already in `Directory.find`.
- **Quebec**: 12 TÉ divisions are cities (Québec, Lévis, Laval, Montréal, Longueuil, Gatineau, Sherbrooke, Shawinigan, Mirabel, Rouyn-Noranda, La Tuque, Îles-de-la-Madeleine) with no MRC government: no region place for them. 5 CDR divisions (Francheville, Le Saguenay-et-son-Fjord, Sept-Rivières–Caniapiscau, Minganie–Le Golfe-du-Saint-Laurent, Nord-du-Québec) are census-only; their municipalities belong to real MRCs that the census does not carry as CDs, so the MRC parent must come from the MAMH directory's `mrc` column, not from the census division. 32 duplicate municipality names across MRCs (Saint-Elzéar, Saint-Siméon...), resolved by the directory's 5-digit code, which is the last five digits of the SGC code. Agglomerations (Montréal, Québec, Longueuil), the two communautés métropolitaines, the Kativik Regional Government and Eeyou Istchee James Bay are governments that span MRCs: load them as institutions with `served_places`, not as places.
- **New Brunswick**: the 2023-01-01 reform replaced 340 entities with 77 local governments and 12 rural districts; parishes are gone. The 2021 census geography is pre-reform, so a 2021-based list would be wrong. StatCan's *Interim List of Changes to Municipal Boundaries, Status and Names* up to 2024-01-01 carries the new NB CSDs (new codes and names, new types RDR and MRM): https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/92f0009x/2024001/tbl/tbl01-eng.csv. Recommendation: build NB from the GNB contacts PDF for names and websites and the interim CSV for codes, with no population until the 2026 census.
- **Lloydminster** is one city with two CSDs (4717029 SK, 4810039 AB); **Flin Flon** likewise (4621064 MB, 4718052 SK). One place each, under one province, with the other code as an alias or dropped.
- Bilingual names with " / " exist only in ON (4) and NB (2).
- Renames and mergers since 2021 (from the agent's reading of the StatCan interim lists; each needs an override): QC Mont-Blanc, Clarenceville, Ristigouche-Sud-Est, mergers La Morandière-Rochebaucourt, Plessisville, Courcelles–Saint-Évariste, Lac-des-Aigles, Amos; AB Diamond Valley (Black Diamond + Turner Valley, 2023), villages Caroline and Halkirk dissolved (2025); SK villages Plunkett, Success, Lancer, Sceptre, Arran, Stewart Valley, Prelate dissolved, Resort Village of Pasqua Lake created (2024); PE Darlington absorbed by North River (2023), St. Louis by Miminegash (2024), leaving 57; BC Queen Charlotte → Daajing Giids (2022); NU Cape Dorset → Kinngait, Hall Beach → Sanirajak.

- **Federal**: TBS *Inventory of Federal Organizations and Interests* (GC InfoBase open dataset, OGL-Canada, modified 2026-10-04): https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/a35cf382-690c-4221-a971-cf0fd189a46f/resource/7c131a87-7784-4208-8e5c-043451240d95/download/ifoi_roif_en.csv. 332 rows (277 active), columns `legal_title, applied_title, abbr_en, min_port (portfolio), inst_struct, status, FAA_LRC_inst (schedule I / I.1 / II / III), website` (253 of 277 active have a website). `inst_struct` maps straight onto types: Ministerial Departments 22 → department; Departmental Agencies 52, Service Agencies 3, Special Operating Agencies 17, Departmental Corporations 17 → agency; Crown Corporations 45 → crown_corporation; shared-governance corporations (80), international organizations (16) and parliamentary entities → leave out or `other`. - **Provinces**: one anchor each for the seed (name, domains): Gouvernement du Québec (quebec.ca, gouv.qc.ca); Government of British Columbia (gov.bc.ca); Government of Alberta (alberta.ca); Government of Saskatchewan (saskatchewan.ca, gov.sk.ca); Government of Manitoba (gov.mb.ca, manitoba.ca); Government of New Brunswick (gnb.ca); Government of Nova Scotia (novascotia.ca, gov.ns.ca); Government of Prince Edward Island (princeedwardisland.ca, gov.pe.ca); Government of Newfoundland and Labrador (gov.nl.ca); Government of Yukon (yukon.ca, gov.yk.ca); Government of the Northwest Territories (gov.nt.ca); Government of Nunavut (gov.nu.ca). - **Provincial agency lists**: only Alberta (Public Agency List, 271 agencies, https://public-agency-list.alberta.ca/, searchable, no export seen) and Quebec (quebec.ca ministères et organismes page; a codes/names CSV on Données Québec with no websites) are close to machine-readable; BC, SK, MB, NB, NS, NL, NT are HTML pages (links in the detailed notes); PE, YT, NU are bot-blocked. None is as good as Ontario's TBS XLSX below. Defer these.
- **First Nations** (note only): ISC's First Nations Location open dataset (band number, name, office point; CSV zip at data.sac-isc.gc.ca) and First Nation Profiles (619 bands, websites per page, no bulk file).

Platforms to add for the rest of Canada (from memory, not researched): seao.ca (Quebec tenders), bcbid.gov.bc.ca, purchasingconnection.ca (Alberta), sasktenders.ca, nbon.gnb.ca, procurement.novascotia.ca. The government-run ones are official rather than platforms and may belong on the trusted side instead.

### 3.4 Loader notes for Ontario

1. data.ontario.ca filenames carry dates and change (school boards monthly, agencies per update, libraries yearly). The `Source.url` pins one release, which is what the hash wants; note the CKAN resource id in the module so the next release is one URL swap.
2. Encodings: school boards ISO-8859-1; FIPPA, OPP and salary files UTF-8 with BOM; FIPPA and service managers embed HTML in cells (reuse `ANCHOR` and `repair_mojibake`).
3. GeoHub layers are ArcGIS REST; the loader's JSON format reads the `query?...&f=json` response as records. The XLSX format exists; the TBS agencies sheet has its header on row 3.
4. e-Laws content is only reachable as legacy `.doc` files, which the loader cannot read: Reg. 553 (health unit areas) and O. Reg. 135/24 (OPP boards) are references for a hand-written mapping, not sources.

## 4. Suggested order

1. **United States seed + places** (`countries/seeds/united_states.py`, `imports/lists/us_places.py`): SUB-EST2025 for codes, FUNCSTAT and population; the 2020 ANSI files for CLASSFP; Govt_Units for legal names and websites; the .gov registry to pre-trust domains. Overrides for the twelve exception classes above. Then `us_school_districts.py` from NCES (19,637 rows, 16,957 websites) because it is the single biggest well-formed list with websites.
2. **Ontario quick wins**, each a small module on the existing loader: provincial agencies (XLSX, 137, websites), FIPPA hospitals + colleges + universities (one source, 190 rows with websites), school boards (85), libraries (about 290), service managers / DSSABs (10 + 37 roles), public health units (29, HTML).
3. **Quebec places** (the one province whose directory is as good as Ontario's: daily CSV, codes that join the census exactly, 1,099 websites, MRC parents), with the census readers factored out of `ontario_places.py`.
4. **Federal institutions** from the TBS inventory (277 active, 253 websites), with Canada's government and domains added to the seed.
5. The other provinces in the order of directory quality: MB and NB (PDFs with websites), NT (HTML with websites), then NS, BC, AB, NL, PE, SK, YT, NU (legal names but no official websites: `find_homepage` does the rest).
